Alaguli Mane

The ancient sowing-and-counting game — Pallanguzhi — reborn in glowing 3D. Sow the seeds around fourteen pits, chain the relays, and capture every four. No dice. Only counting.

Counting made visible

Every pit shows its seed count and the sowing plays out seed by seed — the game was a teaching tool for arithmetic, and the rebuild honours that.

Three worlds, one board

The Malnad home board, a modern Ulita savings reading, and the Suggi harvest tale — each re-reads counting as maths, money, or patience.

Glowing 3D & a sharp AI

Bloom-lit seeds in carved bowls, mobile-first touch, and a minimax opponent that plans the fours several moves ahead.
