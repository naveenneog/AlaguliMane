Alaguli Mane

the sowing & counting game (Pallanguzhi)

Two rows of seven pits, six seeds each. Sow counterclockwise; whenever a pit holds four, it is captured. Chain the sowing, count ahead, and gather the larger harvest. No dice — pure counting.
